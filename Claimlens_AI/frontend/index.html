AI-Powered Motor Insurance Evidence Intelligence

Instant evidence extraction, contradiction detection, and policy-grounded decision support for insurance investigators.

Why Traditional Claim Review Fails

Insurance claim investigators face fragmented documents, delayed notices, and buried policy exclusions.

📄 Scattered Evidence 📁

Crucial facts are buried across police diary entries, handwritten garage quotes, and driver statements with zero unified cross-referencing.

⚠️ Unnoticed Contradictions ⚡

Subtle date shifts between the claim form and the FIR (e.g. 12th Aug vs 14th Aug) frequently escape manual review, allowing fraudulent timelines to slip through.

📜 Dense Policy Exclusions ⚖️

Determining whether commercial passenger use, unlicensed drivers, or delayed notice voids coverage requires time-consuming manual clause comparison.

How ClaimLens_AI Solves It

An evidence-first, deterministic + RAG investigation workflow.

01. Ingestion

Multi-Document Parsing

Reads actual PDF, DOCX, and TXT files page-by-page, retaining source citations and exact snippets.

02. Detective

Contradiction Matrix

Cross-compares dates, vehicle registration numbers, damage locations, and amounts across all submitted files.

03. RAG Retrieval

Policy Clause Verdicts

Vector search matches claim facts to policy sections, assigning verified verdicts: Supported, Blocked, or Uncertain.

04. Human-in-the-Loop

Independent Decision

AI recommendation and human investigator decision remain separate fields with complete audit history.
